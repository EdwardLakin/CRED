# Inspection Integrity Audit

CRED supports documentation integrity by keeping evidence capture, human-authored findings, human review, and export approval separate.

## Product boundary

CRED is not an automated inspection engine. It does not diagnose, certify, auto-approve, or replace inspector judgment. Photos and other uploads are evidence records; findings and recommendations must come from technician-authored notes, verified fields, source documents, or human review.

## Report source hierarchy

1. Technician-authored notes, captions, measurements, and reviewed findings.
2. OCR/text extracted from uploaded forms and source documents.
3. Verified report fields and selected report context.
4. Evidence attachments as supporting records.

## Export integrity

Reports require human review and approval before delivery. Printable, email, share, and saved exports should communicate professional documentation assembled from captured evidence and approved content.
